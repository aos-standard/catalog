@neuronto a run of the 13 EIP-712 vectors (document version 2, sha256 `b21170d8…fd86fd`), with a verifier written from `specs/extensions/extension-offer-and-receipt.md` at `4241d56`. The types and domain come from the spec, never from the file. No Ethereum library is used: Keccak-256, secp256k1 recovery and the EIP-712 encoding are my own code.

**13/13 accept/reject.** The domain separators and type hashes equal your `constants`, and the four valid artifacts' struct hashes equal `structHash`. OpenSSL 3.6.1 verified (r, s) over the same digests with the recovered keys, and derived your `signing.address` from the published seed. Like your `recovery_note`, the chainId-8453, altered-amount and wrong-case-name vectors come out here as "recovered signer is not the configured one", which this verifier reports at `authorization` rather than `signature`.

Two things from the run:

1. **The spec says both things about omitted optional fields.** §4.3 and §5.3 require signers to set `validUntil` to `0` and `transaction` to `""`, and §4.5 step 3 says the payload is used as transmitted, so omission fails. But the §6 JSON schemas leave both fields out of `required`, so the omitted payload is schema-valid there. Your two `*_omitted_*` vectors take the first reading, and so does this verifier (set after seeing them). Listing both fields as required in the §6 `eip712` schemas would make the spec say one thing.
2. **Only the receipt omission vector separates that reading.** A lax reader that fills an omitted field with its empty value, then checks the signature, accepts `receipt_optional_field_omitted_not_empty`, because its signature is over `transaction: ""`. It still rejects `offer_optional_field_omitted_not_zeroed`, because that signature does not verify with `validUntil: 0`. An offer counterpart signed over `validUntil: 0`, with the field then dropped, would catch the lax reader on the offer side too.

Other lax variants are each caught by this set: chainId from the payment network (by every valid artifact and the 8453 vector), types taken from the artifact (by the malicious-schema vector), any v byte, signature without authorization, and refusing expired offers. The limit on independence: `expect` and `reject_at` were on screen while I read the input shape.
