## v2 at the new pin: 48 / 50

v2's point test is sentence 4's `[8]P` only, so the two mixed-order key vectors

## What this does and does not show

- Sentence 9 is implementable from its text with ordinary curve arithmetic; a
  reader that already decodes points for sentence 4 needs only a scalar
  multiplication.
- As with v2 and sentences 5–8, v3 was written after the sentence and its
  vectors were public. 50/50 shows the sentence is implementable from its
  text, not that a cold reader would arrive at it.
- The wording issue omega flagged (`[8L]A` never fires) is real for a reader that
  takes "group order" literally; this reader used `L` as the base point's order
  from the start, so the `5abe7a6` fix did not change its result. That is a
  property of one reading, not evidence that the first wording was harmless.
- Nothing here measures the library's choice of equation; only that the
  reader's result no longer depends on it.
