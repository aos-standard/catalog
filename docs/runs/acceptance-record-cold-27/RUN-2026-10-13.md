## v2 at the new pin: 46 / 46

v2 applies the eight sentences and nothing else. Sentence 4 is implemented with
this file's own Ed25519 point arithmetic (decode per RFC 8032 §5.1.3, three
doublings, compare with the neutral element) for the key and for `R`; signature
verification itself still goes through `cryptography`. Self-checks: the base
point is not small-order; the identity, the `y = -1` point and one order-8 point
are.

If the "Independent implementations" row moves to this pin, it should point at
v2 and say that v1 is the reader the sentences were written against.

## What this does not establish

As in `RUN.md`. Additionally: v2 was written after the sentences and the
vectors were public, so its 46/46 shows that the sentences are implementable
from their text, not that a cold reader would arrive at them.
